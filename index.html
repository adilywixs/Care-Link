What staff get

One screen, 40 patientsHeart rate, SpO₂, movement and device status for every patient, with colors for stable, attention, critical and offline.
Alerts with a reasonA visual banner and a sound show the patient, the room and why the alert fired. The nurse acknowledges it, and it stays in the history.

Try it nowThe dashboard runs a live simulation. Start a scenario, such as a possible fall, and watch every page update.

How It Works

Four steps from the patient’s wrist to the nurse’s screen.

1. Patient wears a deviceA connected wearable is assigned to the patient.
2. Data is collectedHeart rate, SpO₂ and movement.
3. CARE LINK analyzesThe platform checks incoming data against demo thresholds and motion patterns.
4. Staff are alertedVisual and audio alerts appear for potentially dangerous changes.
